FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How does Prism split expenses?

Prism automatically divides expenses based on your group settings — equally, by percentage, or by custom amounts. Just add the expense and we calculate who owes what instantly.

Is my financial data secure?

Absolutely. Prism uses bank-level encryption to protect your data. We never share your information with third parties, and you can delete your data at any time.

Can I use Prism with my existing payment apps?

Yes. Prism integrates with Venmo, PayPal, Zelle, and other payment platforms. You can track expenses in Prism and settle up through whichever app your group prefers.

Is Prism free to use?

Prism has a free tier that includes unlimited groups and basic expense tracking. Our premium plan adds smart categorization, currency conversion, detailed reports, and priority support.

Can I use Prism for business expenses?

While Prism is designed for shared personal expenses, many small teams use it for project budgets and client lunches. For enterprise needs, check out our Teams plan.

What happens if someone doesn't pay?

Prism sends gentle, automatic reminders to group members with outstanding balances. You can customize reminder frequency and even add friendly notes to keep things smooth.
