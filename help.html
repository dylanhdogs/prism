FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to the most common questions.

Is Prism really free?
Yes! Prism is completely free to use for all core features. There are no hidden fees or subscription charges. We believe shared expense tracking should be accessible to everyone.
How are expense splits calculated?
By default, expenses are split equally among all group members. You can also customize splits by entering specific amounts or percentages for each person. Prism automatically calculates each person's share and updates the group balances in real time.
Can I edit or delete an expense after adding it?
Yes. You can edit or delete any expense you created. Group members can see the edit history for transparency. If an expense is deleted, the group balances are automatically recalculated.
How do I invite someone to my group?
Go to your group page and click the "Invite" button. You can generate a shareable invite link and send it to anyone. They'll be able to join with just one click — no account required upfront.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. Prism uses Supabase for secure authentication and database storage. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never share your data with third parties, and you can delete your account and all associated data at any time.
Can I use Prism on my phone?
Prism is a web-based application that works seamlessly on any device with a browser — desktop, tablet, or phone. The responsive design adapts to your screen size so you can manage your expenses on the go.
How do I delete my account?
You can delete your account from the settings page. This will permanently remove all your personal data, including groups and expenses you've created. Make sure to settle any outstanding balances before deleting your account.
