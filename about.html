About Us

Making shared expenses simple for everyone.

Prism was built to eliminate the awkwardness and complexity of splitting costs with friends, roommates, and loved ones.

Our Story

Born from a real problem.

Every roommate, every group trip, every shared dinner — someone ends up tracking IOUs in a messy notes app or a forgotten spreadsheet. We've all been there.

Prism started as a side project by a small team who wanted to build the tool they wished existed: something fast, fair, and frictionless. No manual math. No awkward "hey you still owe me" texts. Just clarity.

What began as a weekend prototype quickly turned into something bigger as friends, family, and strangers started asking for access. Today, Prism helps hundreds of groups stay on top of their shared expenses without the headache.

💎
🔒
Our Mission

Fairness without the friction.

We believe money between friends shouldn't damage relationships. Our mission is to make shared expense management so simple and transparent that you never have to think about it.

Every feature — from real-time balance tracking to automated settlement suggestions — is designed to answer one question instantly: "What do I owe, and who do I owe it to?"

No ads. No upsells. No selling your data. Just a tool that works, built by people who actually use it.

Privacy First

Your data belongs to you.

Prism is built on a foundation of trust. We use industry-standard encryption, never share your data with third parties, and give you full control over your information.

Every group is isolated. Every expense is visible only to the people you choose. When you leave a group, your data leaves with you.

We don't track you. We don't sell you. We just help you split the bill.
